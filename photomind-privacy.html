PhotoMind Smart Photos Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026
App: PhotoMind Smart Photos
Bundle identifier: dev.photomind.PhotoMind
Developer: Chris Wu
Contact: chris5100703@icloud.com

PhotoMind does not upload, transmit, or store users' photos, videos, or files on external servers.
All photo analysis, duplicate detection, similarity detection, and organization planning are performed locally on the user's Mac.

1. Introduction

PhotoMind Smart Photos ("PhotoMind") is a local-first macOS app that helps users understand and organize their personal photo and video collections. This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoMind accesses and processes information when you use the app.

PhotoMind does not require an account or sign-in. It does not provide cloud storage or cloud-based photo analysis.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

PhotoMind does not collect or transmit:

  • Your photos, videos, or other files;
  • File names, folder paths, thumbnails, metadata, hashes, or analysis results;
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information;
  • Location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing history;
  • Usage analytics, advertising data, or tracking data; or
  • Payment card details, Apple ID credentials, or account passwords.

PhotoMind does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or data brokers.

3. Local Photo and Video Processing

PhotoMind processes photos and videos only on your Mac. Local processing may include file scanning, metadata reading, thumbnail generation, exact duplicate detection, visual similarity detection, photo relationship analysis, review decisions, and organization planning.

PhotoMind does not send photos, videos, thumbnails, metadata, hashes, similarity results, review decisions, or organization plans to PhotoMind servers or any external analysis service.

4. File Access Permission

PhotoMind accesses only folders you choose through the macOS file selection panel. macOS provides this access through its sandbox and security-scoped permissions. PhotoMind stores a local security-scoped bookmark so it can reopen an authorized folder after you relaunch the app.

PhotoMind reads files in authorized folders to scan and analyze your media. PhotoMind does not automatically remove or organize files. If you explicitly review and confirm an organization plan, PhotoMind may move the selected files into a dedicated PhotoMind Recycle folder using its reversible organization workflow. You remain in control of each organization action and can use PhotoMind's available restore controls while the corresponding recycle files remain available.

You can revoke access by changing macOS permissions, removing the folder authorization, or uninstalling the app. Revoking access does not modify the original files in your selected folders.

5. Data Storage and Security

PhotoMind stores its catalog, local indexes, thumbnails, hashes, analysis results, review decisions, organization history, preferences, and limited usage counts locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your Mac. Limited usage records describe app stages, durations, and item counts only; they do not contain file names, paths, or photo content and are never transmitted.

Local diagnostic reports contain app status and counts rather than photo content or full file paths. PhotoMind does not send diagnostic reports automatically. A report leaves the app only if you deliberately copy or export it and then choose to share it yourself.

You can remove PhotoMind's local indexes and settings by deleting the app and its local app data. This does not automatically delete your original photo and video folders or files already placed in PhotoMind Recycle.

6. Purchase Information (Apple StoreKit)

PhotoMind may offer a one-time PhotoMind Pro purchase through Apple StoreKit. Apple processes the purchase, payment, receipt, and purchase restoration through the App Store. PhotoMind uses StoreKit product and verified entitlement status only to display purchase information and unlock eligible Pro functionality.

PhotoMind does not receive or store your payment card number, Apple ID password, or other payment credentials. Apple's handling of App Store transactions is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

7. Third-party Services

PhotoMind does not use third-party cloud photo processing, advertising services, analytics services, social login, or cloud storage. The only external service used by the app is Apple's App Store and StoreKit for product information, purchases, entitlement verification, and purchase restoration.

PhotoMind also uses Apple system frameworks for local media and image processing. These frameworks run as part of macOS and are not used to upload your media to PhotoMind or third-party servers.

8. User Rights

You remain in control of your files and local PhotoMind data. You may:

  • Choose which folders PhotoMind may access;
  • Decline or revoke folder access;
  • Review and confirm organization decisions before files are moved;
  • Use available restore controls for eligible PhotoMind Recycle items;
  • Delete PhotoMind's local app data and indexes from your Mac; and
  • Manage or request support for App Store purchases through Apple.

Because PhotoMind has no account system and does not maintain user data on an external server, there is no remote account profile or cloud photo collection for PhotoMind to access, export, or delete.
