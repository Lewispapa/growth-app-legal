2. What PhotoMind Does

PhotoMind is a local-first photo and video organization utility. It can help you:

  • Find exact duplicate photos and videos;
  • Find visually similar photos;
  • Review candidate results;
  • Choose content to keep or remove;
  • Create an organization plan; and
  • Carry out an organization action that you explicitly confirm.

PhotoMind is an organization aid, not a cloud backup or guaranteed data-recovery service. It does not decide automatically what you should delete.

  4. Review the organization or cleanup preview; and
  5. Explicitly confirm the final action.

You are responsible for checking the preview and the choices you explicitly confirm. If a result is uncertain or an item is important, keep the item and review it separately before taking action.

4. Photo Safety, Backups, and iCloud Photos

Before organizing irreplaceable or important photos or videos, you should keep an independent backup that is separate from the library or folder being organized.

iCloud Photos is a synchronization service and should not be treated as an independent backup of the same library. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting an item on one device can synchronize that deletion to other devices signed in with the same Apple Account.

5. Platform-Specific Organization and Recovery

macOS. After you review and explicitly confirm an organization plan, PhotoMind uses its controlled PhotoMind Recycle workflow. Selected files are moved into PhotoMind Recycle, and PhotoMind provides its own available history and restore controls while the corresponding recycle files remain available. You should still keep an independent backup of irreplaceable files.

iPhone and iPad. After you review and explicitly confirm a cleanup, PhotoMind asks Apple Photos through PhotoKit to perform the deletion. Apple Photos manages deleted items. They usually enter the Recently Deleted collection and are generally recoverable for about 30 days, but availability and recovery depend on Apple Photos, your settings, available iCloud storage, actions such as permanent deletion, and other Apple platform behavior. PhotoMind does not guarantee that any particular item will remain recoverable for a specific period.

PhotoMind does not promise perfect duplicate detection, guaranteed similarity detection, or 100% accuracy. Always review results and the final plan before confirming an organization action.
